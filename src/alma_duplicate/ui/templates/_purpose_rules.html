Scope and other observation types

Fixed single pointing uses supported 7m and 12m array evidence; TP does not create a position branch. Retrieval radius is separate from the scientific position test.

Appendix A identifies moving objects by name. For mosaics, more than 50% of proposed pointings must fall within the half-power beam area covered by the other observation; this is not an overlapping map-area percentage. These rules do not imply that moving-target or mosaic assessment is implemented here. Solar observations are exempt.

Available methods and missing evidence are shown in the report.
