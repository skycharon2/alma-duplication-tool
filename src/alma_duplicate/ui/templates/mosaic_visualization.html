How to interpret this view

Teal crosses identify submitted pointings; dashed magenta circles are *display-only* proposed half-power beams. Solid blue outlines are verified Archive s_region boundaries; dashed amber circles are bound Queue beams. White halos mark a selected footprint, never a scientific verdict. A region polygon does not imply a single observed centre.

Optical DSS/Pan-STARRS imagery (via CDS/ESA) is contextual, not radio detections. Use Charts or the linked text report for exact scientific criteria. A missing sky image is not evidence of a non-detection.
